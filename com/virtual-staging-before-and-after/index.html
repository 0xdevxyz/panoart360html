What the difference map shows

Red marks every pixel the AI changed against the original. Furniture may be red; walls, ceiling and windows may not. Thin red lines along edges mean a model nudged a window or a wall by a few pixels, which is the kind of error a buyer notices on a visit and an MLS reviewer may notice on a screen.

Zones are measured per image: the top 42 % of the frame for the ceiling, and the window area. If a zone exceeds 20 % the image is restaged instead of shipped. A full sample is on the proof page (labels on the maps are German: oben = top, fenster = window).

Questions

Are these real client listings?

No. They are demo objects from our own test runs, mostly on stock photos under the Unsplash licence. We say so on this page and on the home page. Client examples follow when clients agree in writing.

Why does the proof show some red on the furniture and window zone?

Furniture is supposed to be red, because it is new. In the window zone, furniture in front of the glass shows up as change. What matters is the share and the shape: filled shapes that match furniture are fine, thin outlines along frames are not.
