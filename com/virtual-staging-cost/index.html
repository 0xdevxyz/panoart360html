What moves the price

  • Rooms and styles. One style across all rooms is the default. A second style for the same rooms costs 50 % of the pack.
  • Resolution. Chat exports from AI tools are often around 2,000 pixels wide. We deliver at least the original size, up to 4k.
  • Corrections. One round is included. A reworked room after that is quoted.
  • Speed. 24 hours is standard, 6 hours costs 50 % more.
  • Complexity. Renovation previews cost more than furniture because kitchens and bathrooms have to be rebuilt in several masked passes.

What a very low price often leaves out

A $10 offer and a $32 offer can look the same in a thumbnail. Ask these before you compare:

  • Is the room structure checked against your original? AI models quietly move windows, redraw ceilings and change walls. We measure every image and do not ship it if a zone exceeds the threshold. More on that in the checklist.
  • Is the disclosure on the image and in the metadata? MLS rules and California AB 723 expect it.
  • Do you receive the unaltered original? You are asked to publish it next to the staged image in many markets.
  • What happens if a portal rejects the image? Ours is a free fix or a refund of the image price, capped at the order value.

Virtual versus physical staging

Physical staging means renting furniture, a crew to place it and a photo shoot after the install, usually billed per month while the property is listed. Virtual staging is billed once per image and can be ready the next morning. It does not replace a viewing: the furniture is not there, which is why every staged image says so. What it does is let a buyer or guest see the potential of an empty room in the listing, where most of them decide whether to visit.
